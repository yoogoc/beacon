## What's new

- Fixed a macOS update issue that could leave the old version installed after choosing to restart. Beacon now starts its update helper inside the signed application bundle.
- If you installed Beacon from a DMG and are still on an earlier version, quit Beacon, open the 1.4.62 DMG for your Mac, and drag Beacon into Applications. Choose Replace when prompted. This manual update installs the fix for future in-app updates.

## 中文说明

- 修复了 macOS 上确认重启更新后，再次打开仍是旧版本的问题。Beacon 现在从已签名的应用包内启动更新助手。
- 如果你使用 DMG 安装了旧版，请退出 Beacon，打开适合你这台 Mac 的 1.4.62 DMG，将 Beacon 拖入「应用程序」并选择「替换」。手动安装这一次后，后续版本即可通过应用内更新。
