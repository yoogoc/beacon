## What's new

- **Reliable text selection:** releasing the mouse now finishes text selection even when a text area's click handler consumes the event. Moving the pointer afterward no longer changes the selected text. Selection also recovers when the mouse is released outside the window, while preserving the selected range and copy behavior.

## 中文说明

- **修复文本选择粘连：** 即使文字区域拦截了点击事件，松开鼠标后也会结束拖选，选区不再继续跟随鼠标变化。在窗口外松开鼠标后也能恢复正常，并保留已选文字和复制功能。
