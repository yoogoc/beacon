## What's new

- Open a Pod's logs to see the latest 100 lines first. Earlier lines fill in above them while new output continues to arrive. The default view loads up to 2,000 lines of initial history.
- You can scroll up to read while history loads. Both the wrapped and unwrapped views keep your place. For a custom time range, Beacon starts near the end of the selected range.

## 中文说明

- 打开 Pod 日志时，先显示最近 100 行，再向上补载较早的日志。默认加载最近 2,000 行历史记录，期间持续接收新输出。
- 补载时可以向上翻阅，换行和不换行视图都会保留阅读位置。查询自定义时间范围时，会优先读取接近所选结束时间的日志。
