## What's new

- **Duplicate tabs:** right-click a tab to open an independent copy next to it, preserving its cluster, namespace selection, search, filters and sorting. Cluster overview and Files tabs are supported too.
- **Resource actions in details:** hover over the resource icon to reveal a compact, animated action bar with the resource's available actions and permissions. Long menus scroll horizontally and stay open while scrolling or dragging the scrollbar.
- **Clearer kubeconfig imports:** selected files appear in a consistent list with filenames, folders, individual source names and removal controls. Add more files without losing the current selection, and scroll through large selections within the dialog.
- **Missing icon and build fixes:** bundle the terminal and other referenced icons, verify icons against the application's bundled assets, and fix the detail toolbar's compilation with normal application features.

## 中文说明

- **复制标签页：** 右键标签页即可在其右侧打开独立副本，保留集群、命名空间选择、搜索、筛选及排序；同时支持集群概览和 Files 标签页。
- **详情页资源操作：** 鼠标移到资源图标上，带动画的紧凑操作栏会显示对应资源的操作，并遵循权限控制；菜单较多时可横向滚动，滚动或拖动滚动条时保持显示。
- **优化 kubeconfig 导入：** 已选文件使用统一列表展示文件名、目录、各自的来源名称及移除按钮；可继续追加文件，较多文件在弹窗内独立滚动。
- **修复图标与构建问题：** 补齐终端等引用图标，按应用实际打包的资源检查图标完整性，并修复详情操作栏在正常应用构建中的编译错误。
