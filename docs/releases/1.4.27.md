## What's new

- **Clearer resource actions:** copy actions and port-forward choices each use one dropdown entry, with explicit labels for the copied content and remote port instead of repeated, identical toolbar icons.
- **Hover to expand:** hover over either dropdown to open it, move into the list to choose an option, or hover over the other entry to switch menus. Moving away or pressing Escape dismisses the list; clicking an entry also opens it.
- **Distinct operation icons:** retry, resubmit, submit, resume scheduling and uncordon now have icons suited to their actions. The required icons are bundled with the application.
- **Reliable toolbar interactions:** preserve horizontal scrolling, scrollbar dragging, permission checks and deletion confirmation when using the grouped menus.

## 中文说明

- **提高资源操作辨识度：** 复制操作和端口转发各合并为一个下拉入口，菜单中明确列出复制内容和远端端口，不再排列多个相同的纯图标按钮。
- **悬停展开：** 鼠标悬停入口即可展开，移入列表后可继续选择，悬停另一入口可直接切换；移出或按 Esc 收起列表，同时保留点击展开。
- **区分操作图标：** 重试、重新提交、提交工作流、恢复调度和解除节点封锁分别使用对应图标，并将所需图标打包进应用。
- **保持操作可靠：** 使用分组菜单时，横向滚动、滚动条拖拽、权限检查和删除确认继续正常工作。
