## What's new

- **Consistent log level translations:** Simplified Chinese now translates Trace, Debug, and Info in the log level menu. Log toolbars, level badges, and the log entry inspector also follow the selected language. Raw log contents and filtering values remain unchanged.

## 中文说明

- **日志级别翻译补齐：** 修复中文模式下日志级别菜单中英混合的问题，将 Trace、Debug、Info 分别显示为「跟踪」「调试」「信息」。日志工具栏、级别标识及日志详情同步使用当前语言，原始日志内容与筛选值保持不变。
