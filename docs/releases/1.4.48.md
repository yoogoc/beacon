## What's new

- **Complete tab titles:** tabs now grow to fit their full title on one line, with horizontal scrolling when the tab strip fills up. Close buttons keep their size beside long resource and file titles.
- **Stable names during connection changes:** connecting, unavailable, and disconnected tabs retain their resource or page name. Hover a tab to see its full title, cluster, and connection status.

## 中文说明

- **完整显示标签标题：** 标签宽度随标题扩展，保持单行显示；标签栏放不下时可横向滚动。长资源名和文件页标题旁的关闭按钮保持正常尺寸。
- **连接状态变化时保留名称：** 连接中、连接失败及断开时，标签继续显示资源或页面名称。悬停可查看完整标题、所属集群和连接状态。
