## What's new

- Resource lists reload automatically after a Kubernetes watch fails or its resource version expires. Beacon backs off before retrying, and the list can recover even when there are no new resource changes.

## 中文说明

- Kubernetes Watch 断线或资源版本过期后，Beacon 会退避重试并自动重新拉取资源列表。即使资源没有新的变化，列表也能恢复同步。
