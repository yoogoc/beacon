## What's new

- **Confirm search results with Enter:** after typing in resource-list namespace, field, label, column or saved-filter menus, press Enter to select the first matching option. Single-choice menus close after selection; multiple-choice menus stay open and repeated Enter keeps the option selected.
- **Preserve existing selections:** empty or unmatched searches leave selections unchanged. Keyboard confirmation skips fixed “All” entries and disabled columns, and supports translated column names.

## 中文说明

- **Enter 选择首条搜索结果：** 在资源列表的命名空间、状态等字段、标签、显示列及已保存筛选菜单中，输入关键词后按 Enter 即可选择首条匹配结果。单选确认后关闭菜单，多选保持展开，重复 Enter 不会取消勾选。
- **保留已有选择：** 搜索为空或无匹配结果时保持原选择；键盘确认会跳过固定的“全部”入口和禁用列，支持中文列名搜索。
