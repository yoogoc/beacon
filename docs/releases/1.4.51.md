## What's new

- **Less intrusive log previews:** Pod and aggregated log previews now open after hovering over the same line for three seconds. Moving to another line restarts the delay; moving away cancels it. Clicking to inspect a log still opens the preview immediately.

- **减少日志悬浮窗干扰：** Pod 日志与聚合日志现在需要在同一行悬停三秒后才显示预览。切换日志行会重新计时，移开鼠标会取消；点击查看仍立即打开。
