## What's new

- Beacon now keeps local application logs for the current UTC day and the previous 29 days. It removes older daily logs at startup and every 24 hours while running, even when the App Logs window is closed.

## 中文说明

- Beacon 现在保留最近 30 天的本地应用日志。启动时及运行期间每 24 小时，Beacon 都会按 UTC 日期保留当天和此前 29 天的日志，并清理更早的每日日志文件。关闭应用日志窗口后，清理仍会在后台执行。
